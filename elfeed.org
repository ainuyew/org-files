* US Market & Macro News
 * [[https://feeds.content.dowjones.io/public/rss/mw_topstories][The Wall Street Journal (Markets & Finance)]] :us-market: Deep coverage of US equities, corporate earnings, central banking, and Wall Street coverage.
 * [[https://search.cnbc.com/rs/search/combinedrenderer.view?partnerId=2000&keywords=finance&target=all][CNBC (Top Stores / Finance)]] :us-market: Real-time US market movements, breaking business news, and earnings coverage.
 * [[http://feeds.marketwatch.com/marketwatch/topstories/][MarketWatch (Top Stories)]] :us-market: US market commentary, sector analysis, and economic data releases.
* Global Financial Markets & Institutional News
 * [[https://www.reutersagency.com/feed/?best-topics=business-finance][Reuters (Business & Markets)]] :global-market: Global macroeconomic policy, international equities, foreign exchange, and commodities.
 * [[https://www.ft.com/global-economy?format=rss][Financial Times (Global Economy)]] :global-market: International trade, central bank policies (Fed, ECB, BOJ), and broader macroeconomic trends.
 * [[https://feeds.feedburner.com/bloomberg_markets][Bloomberg (Markets)]] :global-market: Fixed income, foreign exchange, macro analysis, and institutional capital flows.
* Macro Economic Data & Official Indicators
 * [[https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/fedhistory/rss.xml][Federal Reserve Board (Press Releases / Monetary Policy)]] :macro-economics: Interest rate announcements, FOMC statements, and Federal Reserve policy updates.
 * [[https://www.bls.gov/feed/bls_latest.rss][U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)]] :macro-economics: Official US macroeconomic data including Inflation (CPI), Employment (Non-Farm Payrolls), and PPI releases.
 * [[https://fred.stlouisfed.org/rss/fred_updates.xml][FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis - Economic Data Updates)]] :macro-economics: Automated notifications when key underlying economic data series are updated.
